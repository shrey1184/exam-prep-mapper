10. Brevity in Communication

### 1.1 Meaning of Brevity
* Brevity means expressing the message in the fewest possible words without losing the meaning.
* It focuses on being concise, to the point and clear while communicating.
* Brevity does not mean giving incomplete information, but removing unnecessary words and repetitions.

### 1.2 Key Points
* Brevity is an important principle of effective communication.
* It helps in conveying the message quickly and clearly.
* It saves time for both the sender and receiver.
* It enhances the impact of the message.
* Unnecessary words, long sentences and repetitions should be avoided.
* The message should be short but complete.

### 1.3 Importance of Brevity
1. Makes the message clear and easy to understand.
2. Saves time for both sender and receiver.
3. Increases the impact and effectiveness of communication.
4. Helps in avoiding confusion and irrelevant details.
5. Useful in professional and business communication.
6. Creates a good impression of the communicator.

### 1.4 How to Achieve Brevity
1. Focus on the main idea or purpose.
2. Remove unnecessary words and filler phrases.
3. Use simple and direct language.
4. Avoid repetition of the same ideas.
5. Use short sentences.
6. Be specific and relevant.
7. Eliminate long and complex expressions.
8. Edit and revise the message to make it concise.

### 1.5 Comparison: Wordy vs Brevity Style

| Aspect | Wordy Style (Not Brevity) | Brevity Style (Effective) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Length | Uses long and complex sentences. | Uses short and simple sentences. |
| Clarity | Often confusing. | Clear and easy to understand. |
| Impact | Less effective. | More effective. |
| Time | Takes more time to read. | Saves time. |
| Professionalism | Shows poor communication skills. | Shows good communication skills. |

### 1.6 Examples

| Wordy (Not Brevity) | Brevity (Effective) |
| --- | --- |
| At the present time, we are in a position to inform you that the meeting will be held on Monday | The meeting will be held on Monday. |
| Due to the fact that it is raining heavily, the event has been postponed. | The event is postponed due to heavy rain. |
| I would like to take this opportunity to thank you for your valuable guidance. | Thank you for your guidance. |
| Please find attached herewith the document for your kind perusal and consideration. | Please find the document attached for your consideration. |

### 1.7 Common Mistakes (Lack of Brevity)
1. Using long and complex sentences.
2. Repeating the same idea.
3. Using unnecessary adjectives and adverbs.
4. Giving irrelevant or off-topic details.
5. Using vague and indirect language.
6. Over-explaining simple points.
7. Using formal or outdated expressions unnecessarily.

### 1.8 Summary
* Brevity means conveying the message in the fewest possible words without losing its meaning.
* It helps in clear, quick and effective communication.
* It saves time, increases impact and avoids confusion.
* The message should be short, simple, specific and relevant.
* Brevity is essential in all forms of communication - verbal, written, formal and informal.
