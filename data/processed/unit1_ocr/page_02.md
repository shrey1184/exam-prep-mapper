2. Meaning and Definition of Communication

### 1.1 Meaning of Communication

* The word "Communication" is derived from the Latin word "Communicare" which means "to share" or "to make common".
* Communication means the process of sharing ideas, thoughts, information, feelings and opinions between two or more people in a meaningful and effective way.
* It involves the use of language, symbols, signs or behavior to convey a message and to ensure that the message is understood by the receiver in the same sense as intended by the sender.

**Keywords:**
* Share
* Exchange
* Information
* Ideas
* Thoughts
* Meaningful
* Effective
* Symbols
* Two or more people
* Understanding

### 1.2 Definitions of Communication

| Oxford Dictionary | "The imparting or exchanging of information by speaking, writing or using some other medium." |
| --- | --- |
| Harold D. Lasswell | "Communication is a process of answering the questions Who says What in Which channel to Whom with What effect?" |
| Wilbur Schramm | "Communication is the process of sharing experience through symbols." |
| C.A. Bovee | "Communication is the process of sending and receiving messages through verbal or non-verbal means." |
| Technical Perspective | "Technical communication is the process of conveying technical or complex information in a clear, concise and effective manner to a specific audience." |

**Key Points**
* Communication is a two-way process.
* It involves sharing of information, ideas and feelings.
* It can be done through verbal or non-verbal means.
* Effective communication ensures that the message is understood correctly.
* It is essential in personal, academic and professional life.

### 1.3 Simple Explanation

* Communication means sharing or exchanging ideas, information, thoughts, feelings and opinions with others.
* It is not just speaking, but also listening and understanding.
* The main purpose of communication is to make the message clear and meaningful to the receiver.
* Communication can take place between individuals, groups or organizations through different mediums.

### 1.4 Example in Daily Life

* Talking with friends.
* Explaining a topic to classmates.
* Giving instructions to a team.
* Sending an email to a professor.
* Writing a report in an organization.
* Using gestures or facial expressions.
* Reading a notice or message.

### 1.5 Communication in Simple Words

Communication is the **bridge** that connects people, ideas and actions. It helps us **share** our thoughts, **understand** others and **work together** to achieve common goals.

* ✓ Communication means sharing information.
* ✓ It involves sender, message and receiver.

* ✓ It can be verbal or non-verbal.
