12. Unity, Brevity and Clarity - Examples and Applications

### 1. Unity
* Unity means keeping one central idea or main theme throughout the message.
* All parts of the message should be related to the purpose and should not include irrelevant or off-topic information.
* A unified message is focused, consistent and easy to understand.

### 2. Brevity
* Brevity means expressing the message in the fewest possible words without losing the meaning.
* It is the use of short, simple and direct language.
* It avoids unnecessary words, repetitions and long or complex sentences.
* A brief message saves time and is more effective.

### 3. Clarity
* Clarity means expressing the message in a clear, straightforward and easily understandable manner.
* It ensures that the receiver understands the exact meaning without confusion.
* It uses simple language, proper sentence structure and specific details.

### 4. Examples of Unity, Brevity and Clarity

#### A. Examples of Unity

| No. | Example (Lacks Unity) | Improved (Unity Maintained) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Our company is organizing a seminar on AI next week. The weather is good and lunch will be provided in the garden. | Our company is organizing a seminar on AI next week. All employees are requested to attend. |
| 2 | Students are asked to submit their project report by Monday. You can also prepare for the upcoming sports event. | Students are asked to submit their project report by Monday. |
| 3 | The meeting is at 3 PM. We will also discuss the annual budget, new hiring plans and office decoration. | The meeting is at 3 PM to discuss the annual budget and new hiring plans. |

#### B. Examples of Brevity

| No. | Wordy (Not Brief) | Improved (Brief) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | I would like to inform you that the meeting has been scheduled for Monday at 11 AM in the conference room. | Meeting on Monday, 11 AM (Conference Room). |
| 2 | Due to the fact that it is raining heavily, the event has been postponed to a later date. | Event postponed due to heavy rain. |
| 3 | At the present time, I am in a position to inform you that your application has been received by our office. | Your application has been received. |

#### C. Examples of Clarity

| No. | Unclear (Lacks Clarity) | Improved (Clear) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Send it soon. | Send the report by 5 PM today. |
| 2 | The meeting will be there. | The meeting will be held in Conference Room A on Monday at 10 AM. |
| 3 | Please arrange for the event. | Please arrange the event in the auditorium on 25th October 2026 by 2 PM. |
| 4 | Inform them about the change. | Inform all team members about the schedule change via email today. |

### 5. Applications in Real Life and Professional Communication

| Situation | Application of Unity | Application of Brevity | Application of Clarity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business Emails | Keep the email focused on one purpose. | Use short and direct sentences. | Mention clear subject, deadline and required action. |
| Reports | Include only relevant information. | Remove unnecessary details and repetition. | Present facts, figures and conclusions clearly. |
| Presentations | Maintain a single theme per slide. | Use bullet points and concise text. | Use clear charts, visuals and simple language. |
| Meetings | Follow the agenda and stay on topic. | Speak briefly and to the point. | Explain ideas clearly and avoid jargon. |
| Academic Writing | Focus on the main topic only. | Use simple and short sentences. | Define terms and present ideas in an organized manner. |
| Team Communication | Share relevant information only. | Give concise updates. | Be specific about task, time and responsibilities. |

### 6. Key Takeaways

* Unity ensures one central idea and consistency.
* Brevity helps in conveying the message in the fewest possible words.
* Clarity ensures that the message is easily and correctly understood.
* Together, Unity, Brevity and Clarity make communication effective, professional and impactful.
* These principles are essential in all forms of communication - verbal, written, visual, digital, formal and informal.
