### A. Important Questions (with Answers)

#### 1 What is Technical Communication? Discuss its scope.
**Ans:** Technical communication is the process of conveying technical, scientific or professional information in a clear, concise and organized manner to a specific audience. Its scope includes academic, professional, business, industrial and global communication.

#### 2 Explain the process of communication with a diagram.
**Ans:**
[Diagram: Sender (Idea) -> Encoding (Message) -> Channel (Medium) -> Decoding (Interpretation) -> Receiver (Meaning) with Feedback loop]

#### 3 What are the elements of communication?
**Ans:** 
* Sender
* Message
* Encoding
* Channel / Medium
* Decoding
* Receiver
* Feedback
* Noise (Barriers)

#### 4 What is the scope of communication?
**Ans:** 
* Personal, academic and professional growth.
* Enhances knowledge sharing and teamwork.
* Helps in decision-making and problem solving.
* Essential in business, industry, education, media, etc.
* Growing scope due to globalization and technology.

#### 5 Why is communication important? Explain.
**Ans:** 
* Helps in sharing information, ideas and feelings.
* Builds strong relationships.
* Improves understanding and coordination.
* Essential for success in personal and professional life.
* Plays a key role in a globalized world.

#### 6 What do you mean by communication in a globalized world?
**Ans:** 
* Globalization has increased interaction across countries, cultures and languages.
* Effective communication helps in international business, collaboration and cultural understanding.
* Use of technology (email, video conferencing, social media) has made global communication faster and easier.
* It requires cultural sensitivity and awareness of global communication norms.

#### 7 What are the forms of communication? Explain.
**Ans:** 
* **Verbal Communication** - spoken words (face-to-face, phone).
* **Non-Verbal Communication** - gestures, facial expressions, etc.
* **Visual Communication** - charts, graphs, diagrams, images.
* **Electronic/Digital Communication** - email, social media, etc.
* **Formal Communication** - official and structured.
* **Informal Communication** - casual and unofficial.

#### 8 Explain the principles of Unity, Brevity and Clarity.
**Ans:** 
* **Unity** - single central idea, all parts relevant.
* **Brevity** - use the fewest possible words.
* **Clarity** - clear, simple and easily understandable message.

#### 9 Differentiate between Unity, Brevity and Clarity.
**Ans:**

| Aspect | Unity | Brevity | Clarity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Meaning** | One central idea | Fewest words | Easily understandable and clear message |
| **Focus** | Relevance | Conciseness | Understandability |
| **Avoids** | Irrelevant information | Unnecessary words | Ambiguity and confusion |
| **Result** | Coherent message | Saves time | Correct understanding |

#### 10 Give examples to show Unity, Brevity and Clarity.

| Principle | Poor Example | Improved Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Unity** | Our company is organizing a seminar on AI next week. The weather is good and we are renovating the office. | Our company is organizing a seminar on AI next week. |
| **Brevity** | I would like to take this opportunity to inform you that the meeting has been scheduled for Monday at 11 AM. | The meeting is scheduled for Monday at 11 AM. |
| **Clarity** | Send it soon. | Please send the report by 5 PM today. |
